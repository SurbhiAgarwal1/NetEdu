## 📊 Existing Solutions (Competitors)

### 1. Offline-First Learning Platforms

#### **Kolibri** (Learning Equality)
- **What it does:** Open-source offline e-learning platform
- **Target:** 2.6 billion people without internet
- **Strengths:**
  - Works completely offline
  - Open-source and free
  - Large content library
  - Used in rural Zambia, India, etc.
- **Limitations:**
  - ❌ No network speed testing
  - ❌ No adaptive content based on bandwidth
  - ❌ No correlation analytics
  - ❌ Requires manual content download
  - ❌ No real-time network awareness

**NetEdu Advantage:** We measure network quality in real-time and adapt automatically, plus we prove the correlation between connectivity and learning.

---

#### **Class Saathi** (TagHive)
- **What it does:** Offline-first classroom response system with AI
- **Target:** Indian schools without internet
- **Strengths:**
  - Works offline with clicker devices
  - AI-powered assessments
  - Bilingual support
- **Limitations:**
  - ❌ Requires special hardware (clickers)
  - ❌ No network quality measurement
  - ❌ Classroom-only (not individual learning)
  - ❌ No online-offline hybrid

**NetEdu Advantage:** Works on any device, measures actual network quality, supports both online and offline seamlessly.

---

#### **eGranary Digital Library** (WiderNet)
- **What it does:** Offline collection of 35M+ digital resources
- **Target:** Areas with insufficient internet
- **Strengths:**
  - Massive content library
  - Works completely offline
  - Established since 2001
- **Limitations:**
  - ❌ Static content only
  - ❌ No interactive learning
  - ❌ No network awareness
  - ❌ No progress tracking
  - ❌ No adaptive delivery

**NetEdu Advantage:** Interactive learning with progress tracking, real-time network adaptation, and correlation analytics.
